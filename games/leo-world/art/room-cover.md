Reference image is ONLY the actual game's art direction and white car design: cozy miniature stylized low-poly driving world, lush sage/olive grass, golden road, coral pink trees, turquoise river, small warm wooden cottages. Turn this into beautiful composed cover key art, not a screenshot UI. Feature a recognizable small pearl-white electric hatchback with large dark glass roof and chunky black wheels on a curving golden road in the lower right, river winding into distant wooded hills, miniature wooden bridge, warm cinematic afternoon sun. Retain handcrafted 3D game aesthetics, do not make a realistic photo or add humans. Left/upper area has an elegant soft deep forest-green atmospheric gradient for HIGHLY LEGIBLE large cream Chinese display typography. Exact main title across two balanced lines: 'Leo 的' then '开放世界'. Exact small subtitle: '把人生，开成一个世界。'. Bottom left one restrained cream outlined pill reading exactly '开车出发 ↗'. Typography large enough to read on a small monitor in a 3D room. Professional premium indie-game cover with clear hierarchy, immersive landscape, green/cream/coral/golden palette, beautifully balanced negative space. Absolutely no HUD, speedometer, menu bars, cards, counters, raw route paths, extra labels, monitor bezel or room around it. Flat edge-to-edge artwork only. Keep important text away from edges, within central 80% safe area.

# Profile Content and Style Brief

## Step 1 status

**Content and style locking only.** No route, React component, CSS or page implementation is changed by this brief. Reference images, assets, implementation and QA require separate approval in Steps 2–5.

## 1. Page goal

Create `/profile` as Leo’s **Career Archive**: an editorial, documentary-style professional record that makes a visitor understand how photography, writing, brand practice and systems work developed into one connected capability set.

It is not a conventional résumé, SaaS dashboard, timeline-card wall or a generic dark portfolio section.

## 2. Primary visitors

- Potential collaborators and employers who need a fast, credible view of Leo’s career development.
- Creative, brand and operations peers who need to understand the range between visual work, events, content and systems.
- Friends and professional contacts previewing Leo’s personal brand site.

## 3. Three things visitors should remember

1. Leo began with photography and gained a durable way of seeing people, scenes and visual language.
2. Creative writing and brand practice turned that observation into narrative, execution and collaboration.
3. Leo now works at system level: connecting people, content, platforms, learning and AI workflows.

- Stages 01–05: concise but complete documentary paragraphs, with specific study / work points visible.
- Stage 06: medium density with clearly grouped delivery work.
- Stage 07: highest density, divided into readable responsibility groups and a system relationship diagram. It is the page climax, not a short summary.
- Bottom: a large capability-evolution statement, not a row of small cards.

## 10. Mobile strategy

- Preserve chronology and all factual content; do not remove stages on mobile.
- Convert spatial compositions to vertical editorial blocks, not generic cards.
- Stage 07 system map becomes a vertical Brand → Content → Platform → System / Team reading flow.
- Large colour blocks remain, but simplify geometry and reduce decorative labels.
- Body text stays readable; no horizontal overflow or hover-only information.

## 13. Three genuinely distinct reference directions for Step 2

### Direction A — Editorial Colour Chronicle (recommended)

- **Colour blocks:** full-width or half-width muted blocks that change by stage; type overlaps colour planes.
- **Layout:** a vertical magazine sequence with asymmetric text/image zones and oversized years.
- **Chapter changes:** every stage changes its grid: contact sheet, photography-book spread, script page, path diagram, archive desk, production board, system map.
- **Buildability:** excellent with CSS grid, typography, simple SVG lines and selected real assets. Lowest risk of becoming generic UI.

### Direction B — Contemporary Archive Binder

- **Colour blocks:** off-white paper fields on dark ink ground, using coloured file tabs and broad side bands.
- **Layout:** an archival dossier where stages feel like edited document pages, labels and evidence strips rather than cards.
- **Chapter changes:** 01–03 appear as archival spreads; 04–07 grow progressively more structural and operational.
- **Buildability:** strong with CSS and real assets; more restrained and text-led than Direction A. Risk: must avoid looking like a literal office file.

### Direction C — Documentary Poster Sequence

- **Colour blocks:** each chapter occupies a tall poster-like colour field with one dominant number, one sentence and a distinct content composition.
- **Layout:** stronger cinematic pacing; the page reads as a sequence of seven connected campaign posters.
- **Chapter changes:** large format shifts, visual labels and system graph climax in chapter 07.
- **Buildability:** visually memorable using CSS colour planes and optional real assets. Risk: reference images must ensure it remains a usable webpage rather than a static poster gallery.

## 14. Recommendation before reference generation

Proceed with **Direction A — Editorial Colour Chronicle** unless you prefer a more archival paper-document feel (B) or a more theatrical poster-led sequence (C).
